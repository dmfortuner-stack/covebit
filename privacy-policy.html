Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 September 2026

Summary: CoveBit is committed to protecting your personal information in strict compliance with UK data protection laws and the UK GDPR. We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use tracking or advertising cookies.

What information we collect

We collect personal data when you interact with our website, specifically:

  • Contact and query forms: name, email address, and any details you submit about your IT support query or order.
  • Bookings: when you book a consultation via our scheduling tool, we (and our scheduling provider) collect your name, email address, and chosen appointment time.
  • Payments: when you pay for a service, payment is processed directly by Stripe and/or PayPal, depending on the option you choose. We do not see or store your full card details or PayPal login credentials — this is handled by the payment provider in compliance with PCI-DSS. We retain records of transactions (amount, service purchased, date) for accounting purposes.
  • Technical data: standard website access logs (IP address, browser type, pages visited) may be collected automatically by our hosting and security providers for security and performance purposes.

Why we use your information

We process your data to:

  • Respond to your queries and provide the IT support services you request
  • Process and fulfil orders, including taking payment
  • Schedule and manage appointments/bookings
  • Meet our legal obligations (e.g. accounting and tax records)
  • Keep our website secure and functioning correctly

Our legal basis for this processing is primarily performance of a contract (to provide services you've requested) and, where applicable, our legitimate interests in running and securing the business, or legal obligation (for financial records).

Who we share your information with

We use a small number of trusted third-party services to run this website and business. Each only receives the data necessary for their function:

Category Purpose What they receive
Payment processors Process payments for our services Payment and billing details (we do not store your card numbers or account login credentials)
Email delivery provider Sends email notifications and confirmations Your name, email, and message content
Appointment scheduling provider Manages appointment bookings Your name, email, and chosen appointment time
Website hosting provider Hosts our website Standard technical/access logs
Domain and DNS management provider Manages our domain and website infrastructure Standard technical/access logs
Business email provider Powers our business email inbox Emails sent to dm@covebit.co.uk

Some of these providers may process data outside the UK/EEA. Where this happens, they do so under appropriate safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses, as required under UK GDPR.

We do not sell your personal data to anyone.

Cookies

Our website does not currently use tracking or analytics cookies. Any cookies used are strictly necessary for the site or booking system to function (e.g. maintaining your booking session).

How long we keep your information

We retain personal data only as long as necessary:

  • Query/contact form submissions: up to 12 months, or until your matter is resolved
  • Booking data: retained per our scheduling provider's standard retention, or until the appointment has passed plus a reasonable follow-up period
  • Payment/transaction records: retained for 6 years, as required by UK tax law

Your rights

Under UK GDPR, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data (subject to our legal retention obligations, e.g. financial records)
  • Object to or restrict certain processing
  • Request a copy of your data in a portable format

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at dm@covebit.co.uk.

CoveBit is registered with the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) as a data controller under registration number: ZC247983. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the ICO (ico.org.uk) if you believe your data has been handled improperly.

Changes to this policy
